Privacy policy

Ninety Mornings for iPhone. Effective October 18, 2026

Ninety Mornings does not collect, store or share any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising in the app.

Where your record lives

Everything you enter, such as your goals, check-ins and notes, is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple account, the app keeps a copy in your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple account. We cannot see it or access it.

Purchases

Ninety Mornings Plus is sold as an auto-renewing subscription (yearly or monthly) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Every purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether Plus is active, so it can unlock its features. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the evening reminder, the app schedules the notifications on your iPhone. They are created and shown by iOS on the device and are not sent through any server.

Widget

The home screen widget reads and writes the same record on your iPhone. It does not send anything anywhere.

Your control

You can export your record from Settings, Export my record, and delete it from your iPhone and your iCloud with Settings, Erase everything. Deleting the app removes the copy on that iPhone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
