Support

If something doesn't work the way you expect, write to odaryukigor@gmail.com and you will get an answer from the person who builds the app.

Restoring your purchase

Ninety Mornings Plus is a yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. On a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them. The yearly plan renews once a year at the price shown in the app, and the app does not send its own reminder before that date, so if you want one, put it in your calendar when you subscribe. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Ninety Mornings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to stop the next payment. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your record syncs through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple account with iCloud turned on and it shows up on the new phone. You can also keep a copy any time with Settings, Export my record.

Deleting your record

Settings, Erase everything deletes your record from your iPhone and from your iCloud. It can't be undone.
